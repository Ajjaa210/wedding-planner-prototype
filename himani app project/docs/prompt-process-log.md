# Shaadikipanchayat — Prompt & Process Log

This is the working prompt/process record for the prototype. The three
ideation prompts below are transcribed from the supplied
`Shaadikipanchayat_Prompt_Log_docx.docx`. Build notes describe this prototype
pass; they are not user-testing findings.

## Project context

- **Problem:** Wedding service details are scattered; comparing providers,
  costs and availability can be difficult.
- **Audience:** Couples and families planning one or more functions, with
  wedding planners as a secondary audience.
- **Intended result:** A useful shortlist based on wedding requirements, with
  users retaining the final choice.
- **Non-negotiable information rule:** Do not invent vendor facts, prices,
  ratings, verification or availability.

## Source prompt evolution

### V1 — establish the app idea

**Prompt**

> Help me develop an app idea called Shaadikipanchayat. I want it to help
> people find wedding vendors, including jewellery designers, clothing
> boutiques, decorators, planners and other service providers. Explain who
> might use it and what its main features could be.

**Evaluation from the supplied log:** This established vendor categories and
the basic contact need. It was too broad to explain a user journey, matching,
or how details would be checked.

### V2 — make the journey practical

**Prompt**

> Build on the Shaadikipanchayat idea for Indian weddings that may include
> several functions. Let users add their location, dates, budget, style
> references and service needs. Include helpful follow-up questions, suitable
> vendor suggestions, filters, vendor profiles, a shortlist and an enquiry
> option. Don’t make up prices, ratings or availability.

**Evaluation from the supplied log:** This connected wedding details to
follow-up questions, filters, profiles, shortlist and enquiries. User testing,
accessibility and vendor-data maintenance remained open.

### V3 — add trust, inclusion and privacy

**Prompt**

> Help me map out Shaadikipanchayat screen by screen. Include onboarding, an AI
> chat, vendor suggestions across wedding categories, filters for location,
> budget, style and date, vendor cards, verification labels, comparisons,
> shortlists, direct contact and a planning dashboard. Make space for different
> regional traditions and budgets. Clearly label AI-generated content, protect
> personal information and never invent vendor details.

**Evaluation from the supplied log:** This expanded the flow and made privacy,
verification and different traditions explicit. The suggestions still need
human review and user testing.

## Prototype build record

### Pass 1 — visible baseline

- **Goal:** Keep the original broad directory idea observable for comparison.
- **Output:** V1 has a simple category directory and example profile cards.
- **Decision applied:** Keep V1 deliberately lean; do not describe it as a
  previously built or tested product.
- **Evidence:** V1 is selectable in the running prototype.

### Pass 2 — brief-led discovery

- **Goal:** Connect user needs to category exploration.
- **Output:** V2 brief captures optional city, date, budget range, guest
  estimate, style, functions and service categories; filters narrow the example
  data.
- **Decision applied:** Allow users to skip fields, change the brief and retain
  control over the final choice.
- **Evidence:** The local browser prototype flow is functional.

### Pass 3 — trust and task completion

- **Goal:** Make the limits of example content visible and provide follow-up
  actions.
- **Output:** Fictional/unverified cues, fit explanations, profile questions,
  shortlist, side-by-side comparison, a local-only enquiry draft and planning
  checklist.
- **Decision applied:** No ratings or synthetic quotes are shown. The enquiry
  draft is not sent, and current price/availability must be confirmed directly.
- **Evidence:** Browser walkthrough and responsive check; no participant
  observation is claimed.
